The CTFDL Mock Draft is live on the site. Every board you and everyone else builds feeds a community projection of Draft Night, and after the real picks are in, every board gets scored.

How It Works

Building your board

1Tap players in the order you expect them to go. It works on your phone too.
2Place at least 90% of the pool, then save. One board per account.
3Change your mind? Edit your board any time until the draft starts.
4Keep your takes to yourself if you want. You can post your board anonymously.

Why Bother?

ADP The public draft order Everyone's boards add up to the Public ADP, the community's projected draft order. It goes live once 5 boards are in.
#1 Closest board wins After Draft Night, every board gets scored against the real picks. The closest prediction takes the crown, and the bragging rights.
vs. Read the captains Captains are drafting from the same pool. Let's see who reads them best.
