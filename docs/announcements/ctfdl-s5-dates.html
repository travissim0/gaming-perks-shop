How Scoring Works This Season

Standings are decided by points, not win-loss record. There are two kinds of matches.

RS · Regular SeasonYour official schedule. Mandatory. Worth the most.
FS · Free ScheduledOptional matches any two captains agree to play. Worth less, but they count.
ResultRSFS
Win in under 30 minutes305
Win in overtime (30–45 min)274
Win in double overtime (45+ min)243
Played and lost61
Forfeit loss00

Why we're doing it this way. We want squads playing. More matches, more reps, more reasons to log in. FS gives you a way to play more games that actually matter without adding to the mandatory schedule. Win fast and you get max points. Play and lose and you still get something for showing up. Forfeit and you get nothing. Activity gets rewarded. Hiding doesn't.

FS won't carry you. One RS result is a 24-point swing. It takes five or six FS wins to make that up. But a squad that stays active and wins its FS matches can absolutely climb past a squad that only shows up for its schedule.

FS Limits

2FS matches per squad per week, max.
1FS match per week against the same squad, max.
3FS matches per season against the same squad, max.
✓FS needs a ref or a recording to count. A forfeited FS is a no-contest.
✕FS closes when the regular season ends. There is no FS in the playoffs.

Tiebreakers, in order

RS head-to-head → RS wins → regulation wins → fewest forfeits → fastest average RS win time.
